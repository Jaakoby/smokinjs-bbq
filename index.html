Wood smoked the old west way

No gas. No pellets. Just wood and time.

Every brisket and rack of ribs cooks on an offset stick burner fed with split wood by hand. The fire gets tended all night so the meat is ready when the window opens. That's where the bark and the smoke ring come from, and it's why we close when it's gone.
