    Our story

    Meet Johnny.

    At 18, Johnny Barry spent most of each year working on an off-grid farm where the hired cooks were vegetarian. Meat never made the menu. So every off season back home, he made up for it: steaks, ribs, pulled pork, anything that fit on the grill.

    Eventually he bought his first smoker. He cooked his first brisket at 21, and that's when he knew BBQ was more than a hobby. Years of feeding friends and family turned into catering. Catering turned into pop-ups, and pop-ups turned into the truck. The J in Smokin J's is for Johnny.

    "There's never any shortcuts taken here. Every ounce of seasoning, every slice of the knife, every split that fuels the fire is all out of passion."
    Johnny Barry · Pitmaster & owner
    Wood smoked the old west way

    Oak, salt, pepper, and all night.

    Everything cooks on a 330-gallon direct-flow offset smoker Johnny built himself, burning nothing but oak. The rub is Texas style, just salt and pepper, so the meat and the smoke do the talking. Briskets go on between midnight and 3 AM and cook 12 to 16 hours, depending on size.
